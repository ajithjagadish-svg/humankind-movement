What's the difference between a personal trainer and a movement coach?

A personal trainer typically designs and leads workout programs focused on strength, form, and intensity. Movement coaching starts a level deeper: assessing breath, coordination, and how tissue and skeletal structure are actually working together, since that's usually the real cause behind pain, injury, or feeling unstable, not a lack of strength. Ajith Jagadish is a certified Human Biomechanics Specialist, and the approach is built around correcting coordination first, then building strength and capacity on top of that foundation.
