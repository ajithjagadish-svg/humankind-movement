Client Intake Form

This form helps me understand you before we begin. The more clearly you share your context, the better I can plan sessions that respect your health, lifestyle, goals, history, and current capacity. This isn't about judging where you are. It's about understanding where we're starting from.

Please share information as honestly as you can. Your responses are used only to plan and support your coaching. This form is not for medical emergencies. If you're experiencing urgent symptoms or need immediate medical support, please contact a medical professional or emergency service in your location. Takes about 10 to 15 minutes.

Session Commitment and Rescheduling

Each session requires planning, preparation, and dedicated time. To keep the process respectful and sustainable for both of us, please note:

  • Please notify at least 24 hours before the session if you need to reschedule.
  • If notice is not given at least 24 hours in advance, the session will be considered cancelled.

The purpose of this policy is not to be strict for the sake of it. It is to create mutual commitment, clarity, and respect for the time and preparation involved.
