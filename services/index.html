One-to-One and Group Coaching

Personalised support around health, movement, habits, injury recovery, and self-understanding, one-to-one or in a group. The work begins with understanding the person, not prescribing a plan.

Common questions

Which Humankind Movement service is right for me?

It depends on what you're looking for, not a fixed category. One-to-one and group coaching suits most individual movement, habit, or recovery goals. Postpartum support is specifically for return-to-movement after pregnancy and birth. Neurodivergent coaching is shaped around neurodivergent individuals of any age. Workshops suit groups wanting a shared session rather than ongoing individual work, and corporate wellbeing is built for organisations. If you're unsure, the simplest path is to share your situation directly — the right starting point becomes clear from there rather than needing to self-select from a list.

What's the difference between one-to-one coaching and a workshop?

One-to-one coaching is ongoing, personalised work built around an individual's specific assessment, goals, and pace over time. A workshop is a single shared session (or a short series) for a group — schools, teams, or communities — designed to build understanding and awareness around a topic rather than deliver individualised, ongoing coaching. Someone with a specific personal goal or recovery need is usually better served by coaching; a team or community wanting a shared learning experience is better served by a workshop.

Can I combine more than one service, for example postpartum support plus a corporate wellbeing programme?

Yes. The five services aren't mutually exclusive — postpartum recovery, for instance, is already offered as part of corporate wellbeing programmes through HR wellness initiatives and founder networks. An individual might also do one-to-one coaching alongside their organisation running a separate workshop. What combination makes sense depends on the actual situation, and that's worked out directly rather than being restricted by rigid service categories.

Do all five services follow the same coaching philosophy, or are they separate approaches?

Same philosophy, five different formats. Humankind Movement isn't a single programme applied differently — it's one underlying approach: understanding a person or group before prescribing anything, working with breath and coordination as the foundation, and building capacity rather than chasing a quick fix. Whether that shows up as one-to-one coaching, postpartum support, neurodivergent coaching, a workshop, or a corporate programme depends on who it's for and the setting, not a difference in underlying approach.

Is there a cost difference between the five services?

The individual and group coaching services — one-to-one coaching, postpartum support, and neurodivergent coaching — share the same ₹1,000 intro call as a starting point. Workshops and corporate wellbeing programmes are priced differently since they depend heavily on group size, scope, and format, so there's no fixed rate card for those; cost is worked out based on your organisation's actual context during the initial conversation.

Are any of these services available outside Bangalore or India?

Yes. Most coaching, across all five services, happens online and is available worldwide, not just in Bangalore. In-person sessions are also offered on a limited basis for clients and groups based in Bangalore, subject to availability. If you're outside India, mention that when you get in touch and coaching can be set up entirely online.
