One-to-One and Group Coaching

One-to-one and group coaching gives individuals thoughtful, personalised support around health, movement, habits, injury recovery, self-understanding, and sustainable change, available online worldwide or in person in Bangalore. The work begins with understanding the person, not prescribing a plan.

Coaching may include:

  • Movement assessment and practice.
  • Strength and mobility training.
  • Sleep, food, and recovery awareness.
  • Habit design.
  • Reflection and accountability.
  • Support through injury, transition, or inconsistency.

How sessions work

Most coaching happens online, so it can run from wherever you are, alongside dedicated programs for corporates and communities. In-person sessions are also available on a limited basis for clients based in Bangalore. Whether it's one-to-one or in a small group, the first conversation is about understanding your situation before anything is prescribed, not fitting you into a fixed programme.

Common questions

What's the difference between a personal trainer and a movement coach?

A personal trainer typically designs and leads workout programs focused on strength, form, and intensity. Movement coaching starts a level deeper: assessing breath, coordination, and how tissue and skeletal structure are actually working together, since that's usually the real cause behind pain, injury, or feeling unstable, not a lack of strength. Ajith Jagadish is a certified Human Biomechanics Specialist, and the approach is built around correcting coordination first, then building strength and capacity on top of that foundation.

Is coaching available online, or only in person?

Most coaching happens online for individuals across the globe, alongside dedicated programs for corporates and communities. In-person coaching is also available on a limited basis for clients based in Bangalore, subject to availability — get in touch and mention your location to check what's possible.
