Four pillars

Four strokes, deliberately not joined

Look closely at the mark and you'll notice the four lines never meet in the middle. That's not a rendering accident — it's the point. Each stroke stands on its own, the way each of these four areas needs its own attention rather than one general idea of "health."

Movement

Building a body that feels capable, resilient, and adaptable.

Sleep

Recovery, restoration, and nervous system regulation.

Food

Awareness around everyday nourishment as one part of the whole.

Time alone with self

Reflection, emotional grounding, and building a relationship with yourself.

Movement and sleep are the ink strokes. Food and time alone with self are the terracotta strokes. Four separate lines, four separate practices — none of them stand in for the others.
