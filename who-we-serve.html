Who We Serve

One Philosophy. Individual Application.

Humankind works with people across different ages, life stages, abilities, and ways of experiencing the world. The foundation remains the same: every person deserves to be understood as an individual.

General population

Individuals seeking greater health and self-understanding

For people who feel stuck, disconnected, inconsistent, overwhelmed, or unsure how to create sustainable change.

This work may support:

  • Building a healthier relationship with movement.
  • Improving strength, mobility, and confidence.
  • Understanding patterns around sleep, food, stress, and motivation.
  • Returning to activity after injury or life transition.
  • Creating routines that feel realistic and sustainable.
  • Developing greater awareness of body, behaviour, and choices.

This isn't about forcing a plan onto a person. It's about understanding the person well enough to shape the right plan.

Neurodivergent population

Neurodivergent individuals of all ages

Humankind supports neurodivergent people through a strengths-based and individualised approach. The goal is not to make someone appear more neurotypical. It's to understand how they experience the world and support them in ways that respect their needs, strengths, sensory profile, communication style, and pace.

This work may include:

  • Movement-based exploration.
  • Confidence and body awareness.
  • Play-led engagement.
  • Sensory-aware coaching.
  • Strengths-based goal setting.
  • Support for attention, regulation, coordination, and participation.
  • Collaboration with parents, caregivers, or educators where relevant.

Difference is not deficiency. Understanding is the starting point.

Parents and families

Parents and caregivers

Parents often need support not only with what to do, but with how to understand what they're seeing. Humankind helps families explore behaviour, movement, emotional regulation, and participation with greater curiosity and less judgement.

The aim is to create more understanding, not more pressure.

Organisations and communities

Organisations, schools, and teams

Humankind can support groups through workshops and learning experiences around:

  • Movement and wellbeing.
  • Self-awareness and behaviour.
  • Neuro-inclusion.
  • Health as a foundation for participation.
  • Communication and trust.
  • Sustainable performance without burnout.

The work is designed to help environments become more human.

International clients

Clients across South Asia, the Middle East, North America, and Europe

Humankind Movement works online with clients well beyond India, across South Asia, the Middle East, North America, and Europe. Every session runs online, with scheduling built around whatever time zone you're in. For many people, the challenge isn't a lack of information. It's knowing how to apply that information inside the reality of their own life.

This may include:

  • Desk-heavy, hybrid, or remote work routines.
  • Stress, burnout, or nervous system overload.
  • Returning to movement after injury or life transition.
  • Building sustainable health practices without extreme methods.
  • Understanding neurodivergence, sensory needs, or participation.
  • Finding a healthier relationship with movement, rest, and self-care.

The work remains the same in principle: understanding before prescription. For clients in Europe, Spanish- and French-speaking clients are welcome, and where needed, communication and written follow-ups can be adapted to support clarity and understanding.
